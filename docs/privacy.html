OneBox Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what data OneBox collects, how it is used and stored, and the choices you have. OneBox does not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Data we collect

  • Your OneBox account: your name, email address and a hashed password.
  • Connected email accounts: the server settings and app password you enter, and the mail OneBox downloads from those accounts so it can show it to you.
  • Google account (only if you connect Google Drive): your Google email address and the access token Google issues to OneBox.
  • Settings: your labels, preferences and integrations.

Google user data

OneBox requests these Google permissions, and only when you choose to connect Drive:

  • openid and email: to show which Google account is connected.
  • drive.file: to create a OneBox folder, save attachments you choose into it, and read files you pick with the Google file picker. OneBox cannot see any other files in your Drive.

OneBox uses Google data only to provide these features, and only when you ask for them. It does not transfer Google data to others except as needed to provide these features, as required by law, or with your consent. Google data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train AI models. Your Drive files are not sent to AI providers. People do not read your Google data unless you ask for help with a specific problem, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

OneBox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we use AI

To sort mail into your labels, OneBox sends the sender, subject and text of new messages to an AI model provider (such as OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic or Perplexity) under their API terms, which do not allow them to train on this data. You choose the model in Settings, and you can switch a label's AI sorting off.

How data is stored and protected

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Email passwords, Google tokens and integration secrets are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • Each user's data is kept separate and is only accessible through their account.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Disconnect Google Drive in Settings → Integrations. OneBox deletes its Google token and revokes its access at Google. Files already saved stay in your Drive. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Remove an email account in Settings → Accounts to stop OneBox syncing it. To delete its stored mail too, email us.
  • Delete everything: email us and we will delete your account and all its data within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
