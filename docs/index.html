OneBox Privacy policy

All your email in one inbox

OneBox is an email app for desktop and the web. It brings your email accounts together and uses AI to sort incoming mail into the labels you define.

What it does

  • Connects Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud and other IMAP accounts in one place.
  • Sorts new mail into your own labels, with suggestions you can accept or reject.
  • Lets you preview, download and organise attachments.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, OneBox can save email attachments to a OneBox folder in your Drive and attach Drive files you pick to your mail. OneBox asks only for access to the files it creates or that you open with it, never your whole Drive. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Integrations.

Read the privacy policy for how your data is handled.
